Sent out plenty of CVs and still haven’t heard back? Your experience may be a good fit, but the strongest parts might not be showing up clearly against the JD.

I built a CV Editing skill to help people turn their real experience into a CV tailored to a specific role.

Start by sharing the JD. If you already have a CV, upload it and the skill will help you sort through your experience. If you do not have one yet, it can begin with a guided interview. It asks about the projects you worked on, the challenges involved, your own responsibilities, how you worked with others, and what came out of the work.

A lot of people think they have “nothing to write.” Usually, the useful material is there; it just needs to be drawn out and framed clearly.

Once it has the information, the skill brings the most relevant experience to the front. Each bullet starts with a short summary of the skill or contribution, then explains the action, context, and result—the basic logic of STAR. The goal is a CV that stays truthful to your background while using language that makes sense for the role.

Before it delivers a draft, it checks the two-page limit, wording, repeated phrases, and layout. You can then ask it to put more weight on a certain experience, change the emphasis, rephrase a section, or adjust the format. It will revise the CV around your feedback.

🔧 How to install

Send this link to your AI and say:

“Please install this skill for me: https://github.com/kingsleyluk677-oss/cv-editing-skill”

You can also download the ZIP, unzip it, place the cv-editing folder in your Agent’s skills folder, and restart the app.

🤔 How to use it

Send the skill your target JD and your current CV. If you do not have a CV yet, simply say: “Help me create a CV for a [role] position.” It will start with an interview and continue revising the document based on your feedback.

It is useful for job applications, career changes, academic applications, or anyone who wants a more targeted CV for each role. Hope it helps! 🥹
